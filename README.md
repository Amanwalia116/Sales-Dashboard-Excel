# Sales-Dashboard-Excel
An interactive Sales Dashboard built in Microsoft Excel using Pivot Tables and charts to analyze sales performance, profit trends, customer insights, product categories and state wise sales a clear overview of business performance and helps identify top customers, profitable categories, and monthly sales trends through dynamic data visualization.

Sales Analysis Dashboard (Excel)
Project Overview
This project is an interactive Sales Analysis Dashboard built in Microsoft Excel. It uses PivotTables and charts to summarize business performance and explore sales and profit patterns across years, months, product categories, customers, and U.S. states.

The workbook contains 9,993 sales records covering 2014--2017, with sales activity across 49 states and 793 distinct customers.

Project Objectives
Track overall sales and profit performance.
Compare annual sales and profit trends.
Identify the strongest and weakest sales months.
Compare profit contribution across product categories.
Highlight the five customers with the highest total profit.
Explore sales by state and product sub-category.
Review the yearly count of sales records associated with customer names.
Dataset
The source data includes the following fields:

Order Date
Year
Month
Customer Name
State

Sub-Category

Profit
Dashboard Components
The main Dashboard sheet contains four visualizations:

Profit by Category and Year -- compares annual profit across Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology.
Monthly Sales -- shows sales distribution across the 12 months.
Top 5 Customers by Profit -- highlights the five customers generating the highest total profit.
Customer Count by Year -- displays the yearly count of customer-name records in the source data.
Supporting PivotTable and analysis sheets provide state-wise sales, sub-category sales, monthly sales, and category-wise profit summaries.

Key Insights from the Dashboard
1. Overall Performance
Total sales were $2,296,919.70.
Total profit was $286,409.85.
A total of 37,871 units were sold.
2. Annual Sales and Profit Trend
2017 was the strongest year, with sales of $733,215.19 and profit of $93,439.77.
Sales increased from $470,532.46 in 2015 to $609,205.86 in 2016, and then to $733,215.19 in 2017.
Profit also increased each year, from $49,556.12 in 2014 to $93,439.77 in 2017.
3. Category Profit Performance
Technology generated the highest total profit at $145,455.66.
Office Supplies contributed $122,490.88 in profit.
Furniture generated the lowest profit, at $18,463.31, despite recording substantial sales.
This indicates that Furniture deserves closer review for pricing, discounting, and cost management.
4. Monthly Sales Pattern
November recorded the highest sales at $352,461.09.
December was the second-highest month, with $325,293.54 in sales.
February had the lowest sales at $59,751.26.
Sales were particularly strong toward the end of the year, suggesting a seasonal sales pattern.
5. Top 5 Customers by Profit
The five highest-profit customers were:

6. State-wise Sales
California had the highest sales at $457,687.68.
New York ranked second with $310,876.20.
Texas followed with $170,187.98.
These results show that sales were concentrated in a few high-performing states.
7. Product Sub-Category Sales
Phones recorded the highest sub-category sales at $330,007.10.
Chairs followed closely with $328,167.76.
Storage, Tables, and Binders were also among the leading sub-categories.
8. Yearly Customer-Name Record Count
The yearly count shown in the supporting PivotTable increased from 1,992 in 2014 to 3,312 in 2017. This measure counts source-data records containing customer names; it should not be interpreted as the number of unique customers in each year.

Conclusion
The dashboard shows consistent growth in sales and profit over the 2014--2017 period, with 2017 delivering the strongest performance. Technology leads in total profit, while November and December are the strongest sales months. California and New York are the leading states by sales, and Phones and Chairs are the top sub-categories. The lower profit contribution from Furniture is an area that may benefit from further investigation
